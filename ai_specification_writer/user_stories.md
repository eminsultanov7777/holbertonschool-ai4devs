# Product Idea: SOC Assistant

## Vision

SOC Assistant is a platform designed to help Security Operations Center
(SOC) analysts monitor, investigate, and manage security alerts more
efficiently. It brings important incident information into one
workspace, helps analysts prioritize potential threats, and supports a
consistent incident-handling process.

The product aims to reduce manual work, improve visibility into security
events, and make cybersecurity incident workflows easier to manage,
especially for junior SOC analysts and small security teams.

## Primary Users

-   **Junior SOC Analysts:** Need a clear way to review incoming alerts,
    understand their severity, collect investigation notes, and follow
    incident-response procedures.
-   **SOC Analysts:** Need to investigate security events, correlate
    relevant information, document findings, and track incidents through
    to resolution.
-   **SOC Team Leads:** Need visibility into the team's incident queue,
    workload, response progress, and recurring security issues.
-   **Small Organizations Without Large SOC Teams:** Need a centralized
    and straightforward workspace for organizing security alerts and
    incident-handling activities.

## Key Features

-   **Alert Management:** Collect and organize security alerts from
    connected monitoring tools in a centralized dashboard.
-   **Alert Prioritization:** Display severity, source, timestamp, and
    status so analysts can identify which alerts need attention first.
-   **Incident Investigation Workspace:** Allow analysts to review alert
    details, add investigation notes, attach relevant evidence, and
    record findings.
-   **Incident Tracking:** Track incidents through configurable statuses
    such as New, Investigating, Escalated, Resolved, and Closed.
-   **MITRE ATT&CK Mapping:** Allow analysts to associate observed
    techniques with relevant MITRE ATT&CK tactics and techniques.
-   **Response Playbooks:** Provide step-by-step checklists for common
    incident types, helping analysts follow a consistent investigation
    process.
-   **Team Collaboration:** Support assignment of incidents, internal
    comments, and handoffs between analysts.
-   **Reporting and Metrics:** Provide summaries of alert volumes,
    incident severity, resolution status, and response times.
-   **AI-Assisted Alert Summaries:** Generate concise summaries of alert
    details and suggest investigation steps based on the available
    information. Analysts remain responsible for reviewing and approving
    any conclusions or actions.

## Differentiators

-   **Designed for SOC Workflows:** Focuses specifically on alert
    triage, investigation, incident tracking, and handoffs rather than
    general IT task management.
-   **Accessible to Junior Analysts:** Presents investigation context
    and guided checklists in a clear format that helps less-experienced
    analysts follow established procedures.
-   **Human-Controlled AI Assistance:** Uses AI to summarize and
    organize information while keeping investigation decisions and
    response actions under analyst control.
-   **Centralized Incident Context:** Keeps alerts, notes, evidence,
    assignments, and status updates together to make investigations
    easier to follow.
-   **Scalable Approach:** Can support individual analysts and small
    teams, with the potential to integrate additional security
    monitoring tools as the product evolves.

## Product Scope for the Initial Version

The initial version will focus on a centralized alert dashboard, manual
incident creation and tracking, investigation notes, analyst assignment,
and basic reporting. AI-assisted summaries and integrations with
external security monitoring tools can be introduced as subsequent
capabilities.

## Success Criteria

-   Analysts can review and prioritize alerts from one dashboard.
-   Analysts can create, assign, update, and close incidents.
-   Investigation notes and evidence references remain associated with
    the relevant incident.
-   Team leads can view basic incident and workload metrics.
-   AI-generated summaries are clearly identified and can be reviewed by
    an analyst before use.
